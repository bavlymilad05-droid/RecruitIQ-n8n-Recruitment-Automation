FROM APPLICATION TO ACTION

How RecruitIQ works

An application moves through a transparent, auditable process. Human review remains important for hiring decisions.

01

Candidate applies

A public n8n form collects a CV, job choice, and motivation.

02

Data is validated

Checks form fields and duplicates, then saves the application.

03

AI evaluates

Reads the CV, scores requirements, and suggests an outcome.

04

Team is notified

Updates Sheets, sends emails, and monitors system failures.
